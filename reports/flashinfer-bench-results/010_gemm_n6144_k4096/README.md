# 010_gemm_n6144_k4096

The archive candidate uses an explicit Gluon pipeline and passed all 43 official FlashInfer-Bench workloads at 24.61 µs geometric mean on GB300. Two paired trials measured 24.52 and 24.50 µs versus 45.96 and 45.95 µs for the direct-launch baseline, a 1.875× geometric-mean speedup. The exact candidate took 1.40 s over its first synchronized calls to all 43 shapes from an empty Triton cache, including JIT compilation and execution. The direct baseline took 2.27 s in the same environment. Peak PyTorch allocations during comparison were 0.78 GiB. [Official candidate traces](final-gb300.jsonl) · [Paired traces](final-paired-gb300.jsonl) · [Summary and source digest](final-gb300-summary.json)

The dispatch uses a GEMV for one row, swapped-operand TMA for small matrices, and persistent tensor-core GEMM with separate TMA, MMA, and epilogue warp partitions. Two tensor-memory accumulator slots let MMA advance while the epilogue drains the preceding tile. The kernel writes the full FP16 destination once after FP32 accumulation. The official evaluator passed the unpadded 15- and 35-row cases at 16.26 and 17.66 µs. All 43 Parquet workloads had unique IDs and unique M values, and all generated inputs from the official random-input specification. [Official baseline traces](baseline-gb300.jsonl)

The baseline used GB300 physical index 1 with UUID `GPU-87e04b70-e9c6-0c59-7810-a2a4a6af4f12`, CUDA capability 10.3 and 152 queried SMs. The SM lock was 1500 MHz and active clock samples read 1500/3996 MHz. The driver rejected the immediate memory lock even though its command returned zero. The evaluator was SOL-ExecBench 1.0.2 at commit `a9fa0804c793d438e70850c33fe34426e66d53dd`, with PyTorch 2.10.0+cu130, Triton 3.6.0 and CUPTI Python 13.5.0. The evaluator used 10 warmups and 50 measured calls per workload, cold L2, cloned inputs, preallocated destinations, and first-to-last CUPTI activity timing. A foreign context interrupted the first GPU attempt. The entire baseline was rerun on GPU 1.

The final official and paired runs used physical GPU 0, UUID `GPU-abbc4299-8500-54d7-2a37-3ccac762f30d`, and the same evaluator commit with fbtriton 3.7.1. Both runs queried 152 SMs and CUDA capability 10.3. The selected grid uses the device's queried SM count.

Each sweep paired a candidate with the direct baseline under one GPU lock. The trial logs cover N tiles of 64, 128 and 256, clustered middle-size tiles, the 148-SM local proxy versus 152 queried SMs, and segmented TMA epilogues. The explicit Gluon sweep also compared one versus two resident waves, one versus two accumulator slots, and grouped raster orders at cold L2.
